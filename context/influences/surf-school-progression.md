# Influence: surf-school progression and injury research

* **Stages** follow surf-school and wave-pool level guides (Surf Simply, Lapoint, Barefoot Surf Travel, The Wave, Alaïa Bay): whitewater → green waves → angling → turns → steep take-offs → heavy waves, each defined by observable skills, not years.
* **Water confidence** question (swim 25 m, calm out of depth) comes from wave-pool entry requirements (The Wave).
* **Safety floors:** contest-injury data show overhead waves at 2.4× and reef/rock bottoms at 2.6× the injury risk of smaller waves and sand (Nathanson et al. 2007), hence the overhead and reef rules.
* **Wave shape:** surf-science reviews (Hutt, Black & Mead 2001; Scarfe et al. 2003) tie required skill to peel angle and breaking intensity, hence `power` and `peel`.
* Face height with body references (Surfline) for every size question.
