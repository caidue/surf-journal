# Influence: Surfline

Adopted: **spot names** ([Naming](/spots/naming.md)); surf as a **face-height range with a body descriptor** ("1.8–2.6 m · head high to overhead"); one shared body scale. Surfline's free session log (since 2025) also stores conditions per session, and its "Forecast matches" compare sessions with upcoming forecasts, similar to our [similarity](/engine/similarity.md) matching but per spot. Surfline shows a static ability level per spot; we compute suitability per hour against the [skill profile](/engine/skill-profile.md).
