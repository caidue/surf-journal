# Influence: Magicseaweed-style ratings

Magicseaweed (now part of Surfline) showed solid stars for swell quality and faded stars for what the wind takes away. Our [spot rating](/engine/spot-rating.md) keeps that idea: `potential` (size × period × tide) as the faded bar, `q` (with wind) as the solid bar, multiplicative factors so one flaw sinks the score.
