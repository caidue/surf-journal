# Research

Research reports behind the decisions.

* [Local surf school spot knowledge](local-surf-school-spot-knowledge.md) - Deep research: how local schools decide where and when to surf, per-spot local rules for all 21 spots, wind over the sea from several models, and a plan for a rules engine with validation.
* [Rating errors at La Caleta](rating-errors-at-la-caleta.md) - Deep research: why La Caleta was rated best at 13:00 on 2026-11-14 (a hard mid–high tide factor), why thigh-high waves read "Right in your range", and how other services rate tide and size.
* [Surf journal app feasibility](surf-journal-app-feasibility.md) - Deep research before building: competitors, data APIs, spot data, similarity methods, stack, cost. Some feature ideas were later dropped (feel curves, per-peak segments).
* [Surf metrics, spot data and skill model](surf-metrics-spots-and-skill-model.md) - Deep research: period/energy conventions of surf sites, Open-Meteo model capabilities, spot naming across services, and a skill model with onboarding and rules. Implemented in v1.1.
