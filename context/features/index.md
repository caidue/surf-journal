# Features

User-facing screens and flows.

* [Demo sessions](demo-sessions.md) - Random sessions on real past conditions to try the app.
* [Explore](explore.md) - Region search, map and list of spots, best picks for the day, now or a picked time, similar past sessions.
* [Hourly forecast table](forecast-table.md) - Surf-forecast-style hourly table: spot rating, For you, surf, swell trains, energy, wind, tide.
* [Journal](journal.md) - List of logged sessions with filters, stats and relevance switches.
* [Onboarding: Your surfing](onboarding.md) - First-launch questions that build the skill profile; also the profile editor.
* [Session logging](session-logging.md) - Logging a session on the day's conditions timeline, with ratings, details and how it felt.
* [Settings](settings.md) - Your surfing, units, spots, how old sessions count, categories, phone/offline, data, demo, about.
* [Spot editor](spot-editor.md) - Adding and editing spots, peaks and guides.
* [Spot guide](spot-guide.md) - About this spot, peaks, season, boards, access, warnings, parking, facilities and photos for a spot.
* [Spot view](spot-view.md) - Everything about one spot in tabs, as a sheet over Explore or as its own page.
* [Conditions timeline](timeline.md) - The SVG day chart used to mark a session: For you, surf, swell, energy, wind, tide.
