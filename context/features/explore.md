# Explore

* **Filter panel:** one card (`.filter-panel`) holding everything the list depends on, in one control style. **Top row (narrow, 32 px, a hairline below it):** the area as a **breadcrumb** with a pin icon ("Fuerteventura › North", split from the stored label "North of Fuerteventura"; "Near you (30 km)" stays one crumb), at most 70 % of the row; tapping it opens the area picker below (free text in English, German or Spanish direction words via [Nominatim](/data-sources/nominatim.md), or *Near me* (30 km); closes after a search, Esc closes). On the right the level name as a small soft chip in the band's colour (tinted background and border, e.g. "Intermediate+"; the tooltip adds stage and comfortable size). Tapping it opens the **Rate for** menu ([decision 0021](/decisions/0021-rate-for-another-level.md)): "Me · Intermediate+" (your profile and sessions), then "Someone else" with the 8 levels (band dot, short title, comfortable size), and "Edit your surfing profile →" (Settings). Choosing a level rates everything for a typical surfer of that level (`profileForStage`) without your session history (`useRating`, hooks.ts); the chip turns accent-coloured, reads "Rating for Advanced" ("For Advanced" on phones) and gets an × to go back to you. Texts name the level instead of "you" (list heading, "Best for Advanced", "Good for Advanced", map marker tooltips, the first explanation line "Rated for a typical Advanced surfer …; your own sessions are left out"); the "Edit comfort size" links under size reasons are hidden. The choice lives in memory only (`setRateFor`), so reopening the app rates for you again. Similar sessions stay yours. The last area is remembered (localStorage) and the query in settings.
* **Filters** (second row of the panel, no visible labels): Day as a segmented control (Today / Tomorrow / day after) and **Time** as a matching segmented-style track with a clock icon, turning white like a selected segment once a time is set: "Any time" (the default, nothing set: the day's best windows), "Now" (today only, while there is daylight) or one of the day's daylight hours (from the current hour today). Changing the day keeps a chosen hour if that day has it; "Now" falls back to Any time on other days.
* **Spots for you (one ranked list):** every spot in the area is a pick for the Day and Time filters, best first, under a quiet line "21 spots · best for you tomorrow at 14:00 first". There is no separate Best picks section any more (decided 2026-11-16: the score you see is always yours, for the time you chose). The hour behind each card is the spot's best window of the day (Any time), this hour (Now) or the chosen hour ([Day recommendation](/engine/day-recommendation.md)). Ranking: score for you plus 0.4 (Reliable) or 0.2 (Less certain), so on close scores a well-known spot ranks first ([How sure a rating is](/engine/confidence.md)); spots that aren't for you or have no rating at that hour follow (ordered by spot rating), then spots with no hour (dark now, no forecast). Each card: map thumbnail, name, break · bottom · level, the score for you ("–" when withheld); the **go line** "Go 11:00–13:00 · tide mid, falling" (or "Now", "At 14:00", "Best at" when nothing works); the hour's conditions as **badges** (`CondBadges`, ui.tsx): wave icon + face-height range, swell icon + peak period + direction arrow, wind icon + speed + arrow on the forecast's wind colour (offshore green … onshore red), details in the tooltips; then `PickDetails` (ui.tsx): timing notes (warning triangle like the points against, Any time only: wind turning onshore, models disagreeing), the [suitability reasons](/engine/suitability-rules.md) (✓ for, a small warning triangle against, ✕ not for you), one bullet list (• "Best for you: <peak>" when peaks differ, "Other times today are about as good (within half a point)" on a fairly even day, the explanation lines; `explain` tags each line with a kind: lines about your past sessions get a small journal icon instead of the dot, "Held back by …" the warning triangle like the reasons against), and a footer **"Confidence in this rating: [Reliable]"** (`SureLine`; reasons on hover). No suitability badge: the score already says it. The spot's recommendation card uses the same layout. Cards scoring 0.5 or less have a slightly faded head. A card above the list says when nothing works (or it's dark now, or no daylight is left today). Tap opens the [spot view](/features/spot-view.md) as a sheet; with an hour chosen its forecast table opens scrolled to that hour, with one hour before it (when at least four columns fit) and the following hours in view.
* **Layout:** on wide screens (≥ 900 px) Explore fits the window and the page itself never scrolls (no page scrollbar): the filter panel on top, the map filling the left column, and **only the list on the right scrolls** (to its ends, no bounce; `overflow` on `.list-col`, the page height set with `:has(.page.explore)`). The page footer (data and map credits) moves to the end of the list there. Score markers ([Maps](/architecture/maps.md)). On phones the page scrolls as before and a bottom bar switches List · N / Map; the list is the default.
* **Similar sessions:** "Your past sessions in conditions like today" with % similarity and what differed most, or the nearest ones when none is close enough ([Similarity](/engine/similarity.md)).
* After sunset Explore jumps to tomorrow once (without a notice: the Day filter shows it).
